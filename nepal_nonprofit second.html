Our Mission

Rooted in Seva,
Built on Service

Nepal Seva Foundation is a nonprofit dedicated to lifting communities across Nepal through education, healthcare, disaster relief, and sustainable development. We believe in the power of collective action.

🎓

Education First

Scholarships, schools & training for all ages.

🏥

Healthcare Access

Free clinics and mobile health camps.

🌱

Sustainability

Eco-friendly development that lasts generations.

🤝

Community Power

Local leaders driving local solutions.

Our Work

Transforming Lives
Across Nepal

From mountain villages to urban centers, our projects create lasting change.

📚 Education

Mountain School Initiative

Building and renovating schools in remote Himalayan communities, providing quality education to children who previously had to walk hours to learn.

Progress78%
3,200+ students Learn more →
🌿 Environment

Green Nepal Reforestation

Planting native trees across deforested hillsides, restoring ecosystems and providing livelihoods for local farmers through sustainable agroforestry.

Progress62%
50K+ trees planted Learn more →
🏥 Healthcare

Rural Health Camp Network

Mobile clinics bringing doctors, medicine, and preventive care to villages where the nearest hospital is days away, serving thousands annually.

Progress91%

🏠 Disaster Relief

Earthquake Rebuild Program

Constructing earthquake-resistant homes for families still displaced, incorporating traditional Newari architectural elements with modern safety standards.

Progress55%
640 families Learn more →
💧 Clean Water

Clean Water for All

Installing community water systems and sanitation facilities in rural areas, dramatically reducing water-borne diseases and improving daily life.

Progress84%
15K+ households Learn more →
👩 Women Empowerment

Shakti Women's Cooperative

Vocational training, microloans, and mentorship for women entrepreneurs across Nepal, building economic independence and community leadership.
